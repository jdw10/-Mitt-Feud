START / WIN
Four houses, each with 100 gold, 20 reserve troops, no land, zero checks. All start at Muster without an opening corner reward. Roll for initiative, reroll highest ties; then clockwise player order. Own 16 lots or be the last surviving house to win. There are 30 ownable lots in 8 divisions and four corner spaces.

TURN
Roll two dice and move clockwise. There are no trails or route choices. You may deposit troops or build on owned lots you pass. Resolve your destination: buy unowned land, automatically take an undefended rival lot, or attack/pass on defended rival land. Then reinforce and build on your owned destination. Buy/resolve cards one at a time, or end your turn. Collect income ONCE at your own turn's end.
Original movement doubles allow one extra roll for 20 G, after resolving the destination and before cards. No chained extra rolls. Card movement reopens destination/deposit/build and then returns to cards, without extra EoT income.

One building per lot; one building purchase per movement, including stops while passing owned land. New buildings pay this turn. Captured buildings remain, but may be demolished without refund; a replacement can then be built. Deposited troops cannot be retrieved.
This board uses two divisions of each size: 2, 3, 4 and 6 lots.

COMMITTED COMBAT
When attacking, first choose exactly how many reserve troops to commit. They leave reserve and form a separate attacking force. No reinforcements or second commitment during that encounter.
Both sides MUST roll one die per troop in their force, capped at three dice: 1 troop = 1 die; 2 = 2 dice; 3 or more = 3 dice. Defender uses the lot's stationed troops. Nobody chooses fewer dice.
Sort each side highest first. Compare all corresponding pairs (up to three); ignore unmatched dice. Lower die loses one troop; ties favor the defender. There is NO +1 defender modifier, even for complete divisions.
Casualties come only from the committed attackers or stationed defenders. Spare reserves never join automatically. Roll the next round or retreat between rounds. A defeated attacking force ends the encounter even if reserves remain.
Retreat returns surviving attackers to reserve and closes the attack opportunity for that destination. Victory transfers the lot and stations ALL surviving committed attackers. You may then reinforce in the normal management step. Undefended capture remains free/automatic without compulsory deposit.
Supporting rulings for this version: any number from 1 to the full reserve may be committed; there is no forced one-troop reserve. Survivor return/occupation rules above complete the committed-force system.

CHECKS / REQUIRED PAYMENT
If a required cost is fully affordable in BOTH gold and reserve troops, it must be paid. If either part is unaffordable, take one check and lose NONE of those resources. No partial payments and no choosing a check to avoid an affordable payment. Card purchase price is already paid and is not refunded when its effect causes a check.
Checks do not reset. On the third, eliminate the house and remove ownership, stationed troops and buildings; former lots become unowned. Optional unaffordable purchases are unavailable and do not cause checks.

BOARD / DIVISIONS
Only the outer ring is playable: 34 spaces total, including 30 ownable lots.
Clockwise from Muster:
Top: Guildmarket (2 lots), Sunspire (6 lots), then Levy.
Right: Tidewatch (3 lots), Red Keep (4 lots), then Treasury.
Bottom: Riverhold (2 lots), Rose Quarter (6 lots), then Tax.
Left: Unicorn Vale (3 lots), Lion's Reach (4 lots), then Muster.
All outer lot names and positions are retained; division membership changes to match these sizes. The former 24 inner lots and all branch choices are removed. Owning a complete division doubles building income only; no combat modifier.

CORNERS
Muster: +10 reserve troops. Levy: pay 10 reserve troops.
Treasury: +100 gold. Tax: pay 100 gold.
Landing only; not passing or initial setup. Unaffordable costs use the check rule.

PRESENTATION / SAVES
M = market; B = barracks; T = stationed troops; star = full division.
Pawn/holding colors match the house panels. Animations use the acting house's color; battles use both houses. Actual rolled dice are displayed after the brief rolling animation. The display dice animation uses no gameplay random numbers.
Menu and Rules pause computer turns and animation timers. Manual Save/Load plus automatic saving after every action. Version 3 requires a new campaign. Version 1/2 maps cannot be loaded. The new title uses its own save directory and ring-specific filenames; old saves are left intact.

UPDATE SCOPE / RETAINED RULINGS
The requested update removes the trails, divides eight-lot sides into 2 + 6 and seven-lot sides into 3 + 4, retains the original artwork, and renames the game Mitt Fued.
All established prices and EoT outputs in the table are unchanged. Cards, combat, checks, base income, four players, corners, and 16-lot/last-survivor victory are retained. The 16-lot target is deliberately not rebalanced automatically for this smaller board.
Other retained prototype rulings: corner effects on landing only, no troop retrieval, one building per movement, paid doubles before cards, and persistent checks.
